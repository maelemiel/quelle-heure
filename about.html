About

Time Right Now is a parody with a real purpose. Every euro collected here funds ALLY, our accessibility project: ALLY audits real websites and helps fix them, so the web becomes usable for everyone. You buy the time; we give it back.

The paying-customer counter on the home page is the real number of processed payments, straight from Stripe. We do not inflate it. Yet.

This site is also a deliberate coding exercise: minimal vanilla JavaScript, no framework, no build step, fully static (one tiny serverless function for the counter), and accessibility-first (AAA/AA contrast, full keyboard navigation, reduced motion). View source is the documentation.
